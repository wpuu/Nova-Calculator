# Nova / Utility Factory — Gray-Edge Opportunity Matrix V5

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: opportunity research; NOT a build commitment

## 1. New rule: maximize economic value per AI call

Do not prefer products that invoke AI on every chat message, screen frame, or tap just because inference is assumed free. Even zero-cost inference creates latency, reliability, privacy, abuse and support burden.

Prefer workflows where one AI-assisted analysis can create or protect meaningful economic value:

- recover money already owed;
- prevent a merchant loss;
- identify a profitable sourcing decision;
- identify a claim or compensation opportunity;
- convert fragmented platform rules into an evidence/checklist package.

Agnes remains an internal analysis weapon. User-facing execution should be deterministic where possible.

## 2. Gray / black demand is used as a signal, not copied literally

We may study high-demand behaviors that live near platform or legal boundaries, but should convert them into durable legal mirrors:

| Dark/gray demand signal | Durable product mirror |
|---|---|
| refund abuse / refund fraud | legitimate seller reimbursement audit and chargeback defense |
| purchase bots / queue bypass | price, stock and restock intelligence; no bypass/auto-purchase |
| spam auto-replies | reply drafting / inbox triage with user confirmation |
| account farming / mass accounts | supported multi-account business workflow; no evasion |
| fake reviews / review manipulation | review-risk and authenticity monitoring |
| aggressive scraping | API/user-authorized/public-data intelligence without bypassing access controls |
| regional-pricing abuse | transparent regional price comparison, not misrepresentation of eligibility/location |

Do not build a business that requires credential theft, fake identities, bot-detection evasion, fake claims, fake reviews, ad-click fraud, captcha bypass or unauthorized account access.

## 3. Candidate A — Seller Revenue Recovery (highest current priority)

### Evidence

Amazon FBA reimbursement is already a proven paid category:

- Helium 10 bundles a Managed Refund Service and charges roughly 10–15% depending on plan.
- GETIDA uses performance pricing and publicly states no payment unless money is recovered; current market references show about 25% contingency pricing.
- GETIDA states sellers may lose up to roughly 3% of FBA revenue to lost/damaged inventory, fee discrepancies and related reimbursement issues.
- Multiple 2026 services now sell read-only audits, flat-fee case preparation or lower contingency alternatives.

This is stronger payment evidence than consumer utility subscriptions because the product directly returns money.

### Best wedge

Do NOT begin by becoming a full claims-management company.

Phase A — content/affiliate layer:

1. Grok creates highly specific recovery/fee pages and calculators.
2. User enters revenue/account profile or exports a report.
3. Agnes/rules explain likely claim categories and estimated opportunity.
4. Refer qualified sellers to an existing authorized recovery partner such as GETIDA.
5. Earn affiliate/revenue share.

GETIDA has a public affiliate program with a 90-day cookie and revenue sharing when referred sellers recover money.

Phase B — read-only audit product if traffic converts:

- official Seller Partner API authorization;
- deterministic reconciliation engine;
- identify likely discrepancies;
- produce an evidence pack / deadline / filing checklist;
- seller manually files, or an approved recovery partner handles filing.

### Moat / anti-bypass

A one-time explanation is easy to bypass. Continuous monitoring is not:

- new shipments;
- new refunds;
- lost/damaged inventory;
- dimension/weight fee changes;
- claim deadlines;
- policy changes;
- case status.

The customer keeps the tool because money can leak every month.

### Difficulty

- affiliate + calculator: 2–3/10
- report-upload audit: 4–5/10
- SP-API read-only monitor: 6–7/10
- full claim automation/service: 8+/10 plus policy/operational burden

### Policy warning

Amazon introduced stronger AI/agent requirements in March 2026. Any automated access must follow current Agent Policy and identify itself where required. Avoid using Amazon materials for prohibited AI-development purposes. Prefer deterministic rules for account auditing and keep Agnes outside direct autonomous account control.

### Score

9.3 / 10 for validation.

## 4. Candidate B — Flight Compensation Affiliate / Claim Discovery

### Evidence

AirHelp currently states:

- 28M+ customers helped;
- 3M+ passengers paid / 3.3M+ compensated depending on current page;
- support across major passenger-rights regimes such as EC261, UK261, Brazil ANAC, Turkey SHY, Saudi GACA and Montreal Convention situations;
- standard consumer success fee around 35%;
- affiliate program paying 15% of AirHelp's fee on successful claims;
- current affiliate page shows roughly EUR70–75 average affiliate payout per approved claim;
- 20% commission on AirHelp+ membership;
- 30-day cookie;
- 19+ languages.

This is a nearly pure "borrow somebody else's execution infrastructure" business.

### Product

Build a useful eligibility/education layer, not a law firm:

- airline + route + disruption type + timing input;
- preliminary eligibility estimate;
- evidence checklist;
- country/regime explanation;
- redirect eligible users through affiliate tracking to the claims provider.

### Grok leverage

Programmatic long-tail pages can be produced around:

- airline;
- airport pair;
- country/regime;
- delay/cancellation/overbooking;
- baggage delay/loss;
- missed connections.

Do not publish thin keyword spam. Each page should contain a working eligibility calculator/checklist and clearly state that final eligibility is determined by the claims provider / applicable rules.

### Agnes leverage

Low-frequency, high-value:

- parse itinerary/email/screenshot uploaded by user;
- normalize flight facts;
- identify likely governing regime;
- prepare the user's evidence checklist.

One call may lead to an affiliate payout worth tens of euros, unlike a chat assistant call worth fractions of a dollar.

### Anti-bypass

Users can claim directly, so the information itself is bypassable. Our durable value is discovery + routing + reminders + evidence organization. The strongest model is affiliate lead generation rather than trying to charge users directly.

### Difficulty

3–4/10 for calculators/pages/affiliate routing.

### Score

9.0 / 10 for low-capital validation.

## 5. Candidate C — Merchant Chargeback / Dispute Evidence Builder

### Evidence

2026 chargeback-defense vendors show strong willingness to pay:

- flat SaaS around $149–$499/month;
- success-fee models around 10–25% of recovered amount;
- Shopify/Stripe-focused tools sell structured evidence packs and automated defense;
- TikTok Shop US policy generally gives sellers only 7 calendar days to appeal many chargebacks and requires evidence such as transaction status, product description, proof of receipt, tracking and related records.

The urgent deadline plus direct money loss makes this much higher-value than consumer utilities.

### Narrow wedge

Start as an evidence-pack generator, not autonomous financial/legal representation:

1. merchant uploads order, tracking, customer messages and policy facts;
2. deterministic checklist detects missing evidence;
3. Agnes structures facts and drafts a concise dispute narrative;
4. export PDF/TXT/evidence index;
5. merchant reviews and submits.

First niche could be TikTok Shop/Shopify merchants rather than every processor.

### Why gray-edge

The demand is created partly by friendly fraud, disputed transactions and platform/bank asymmetry. We sit on the merchant-defense side, not the fraudulent claimant side.

### Difficulty

5–6/10 for upload/export workflow;
7–8/10 for deep processor integrations.

### Risks

Sensitive customer/order data; require strong privacy/security design. Do not fabricate evidence or optimize false claims.

### Score

8.8 / 10.

## 7. Candidate E — Seller Pricing / Arbitrage Intelligence

Paid willingness is extremely strong: mature Amazon/eBay repricers commonly charge about $29–$499+/month, with higher enterprise tiers.

However, a direct repricer is already crowded and requires robust marketplace integrations.

Better wedge for us:

- cross-marketplace margin monitor;
- China sourcing cost -> target marketplace price -> net-margin alert;
- inventory/price anomalies;
- "do not reprice below landed cost" risk layer;
- public/API-authorized competitive intelligence.

Do not build purchase bots, queue bypass or anti-bot evasion.

Difficulty: 6–8/10 depending on integrations.
Score: 7.8 / 10 until a narrower wedge is validated.

## 11. New strategic pattern: Money Recovery / Money Leakage Engine

The strongest opportunity cluster discovered so far is not "Android utilities". It is:

> Find money users are legally entitled to, money they are unnecessarily losing, or expensive decisions they are about to make — then reduce the friction of recovery/decision and take a subscription, affiliate fee or success share.

Potential verticals:

- airline passenger compensation;
- Amazon FBA reimbursement;
- TikTok Shop seller logistics reimbursement;
- merchant chargebacks;
- marketplace fee discrepancies;
- sourcing landed-cost mistakes;
- later, other clearly lawful compensation/refund programs.

This model is harder to bypass than a generic information site when it includes continuous monitoring, deadlines, evidence organization and partner execution.

## 13. Build rule

Before a major feature is implemented, require at least one of:

- real affiliate conversion;
- real uploaded-document usage;
- repeated free audit usage;
- seller willingness to connect/export data;
- credible paid pre-order/waitlist signal;
- partner acceptance.

Pages and lightweight tools should validate demand before the Android app or deep integrations are expanded.
